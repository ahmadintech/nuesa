Student Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

NUESA dues can be paid at the NUESA Secretariat located inside the Faculty of Engineering complex, or via approved digital payment channels available on the Student Portal. Ensure you collect your official stamped receipt.

Visit the Resources Page on this portal. Select your department and academic level (100L - 500L) to download free lecture PDFs, past examination papers, and software guides.

Registration forms for hackathons, CAD design contests, and software bootcamps are announced on the portal newsletter and social handles 2 weeks prior to Engineering Week.
