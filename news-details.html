NUESA Annual Engineering Week 2026 Kickoff & Innovation Expo

calendar_today Aug 28, 2026 person NUESA Executive Council schedule 4 min read visibility 1,240 Views

The Executive Council of the Nigerian Universities Engineering Students Association (NUESA), University of Maiduguri Chapter, is thrilled to announce the official schedule for the 2026 Annual Engineering Week & Student Innovation Expo.

This flagship annual event brings together over 2,500 engineering undergraduates across 8 accredited departments for five days of intense technical competitions, paper presentations, cultural carnivals, and high-impact industrial networking sessions.

Event Key Highlights:
  • Hackathon & Software Pitch: AI, IoT & Embedded Systems Solutions.
  • Spaghetti Bridge Structural Contest: Load testing and civil engineering challenge.
  • Keynote Industry Lecture: Renewable Energy Transitions in Sub-Saharan Africa.
  • Grand Awards & Dinner Night: Honoring outstanding engineering scholars.

Event Schedule Overview

Day Date Activity Focus Venue
Day 1 Mon, Sept 14 Cultural Carnival & Faculty Parade Engineering Complex Grounds
Day 2 Tue, Sept 15 Innovation Expo & Spaghetti Bridge Contest Faculty Main Auditorium
Day 3 Wed, Sept 16 Dean's Cup Final Match & Sports Fiesta UNIMAID Sports Center
Day 4 Thu, Sept 17 Keynote Symposium & Career Workshop ETTF Lecture Hall
Day 5 Fri, Sept 18 Grand Dinner Night & Awards Banquet University Multipurpose Hall

"Engineering Week 2026 is designed to ignite creativity, bridge academic theory with industrial practice, and foster unified solidarity across all departments."

Comrade Executive President, NUESA UNIMAID Chapter

Registration for student project stalls, paper presentations, and dinner pass tickets is now open online via the student portal. All class representatives are advised to coordinate departmental participation with the Steering Committee.
