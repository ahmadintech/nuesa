President's Address

Welcome to NUESA UNIMAID Chapter

"Building world-class engineers through innovation, academic integrity, and dedicated student advocacy."

On behalf of the Executive Council and the entire engineering student body of the University of Maiduguri, I warmly welcome you to our official web portal. Founded over 25 years ago, NUESA UNIMAID has grown into a vibrant umbrella body uniting over 2,500 students across 8 accredited engineering departments.

Our Mission

Empower students through technical mentorship, academic past questions, and career placement support.

Our Vision

To produce industry-ready engineering graduates capable of solving complex national challenges.
